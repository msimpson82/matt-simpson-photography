There are events you show up to for the work, and then there are events you show up to because they're the most fun thing happening in Louisville that day. NuLu Bockfest is the second kind. Goat racing, bock beer, live music, a wurst competition, and a few thousand people packed into one of the best neighborhoods in the city. This was a blast to shoot.

![NuLu Bockfest event photography in Louisville](/images/blog/nulu-bockfest-louisville-event.jpg)

## What Is NuLu Bockfest

For those who haven't been, NuLu Bockfest is an annual spring festival put on by the NuLu Business Association. It celebrates the old German tradition of releasing bock beer after winter aging to mark the change of season. The event takes place in the heart of NuLu - Louisville's "New Louisville" neighborhood - right between the two alleys that give the area its character: Nanny Goat Strut and Billy Goat Strut. Yes, the goat theme is very real here.

![Goat racing at NuLu Bockfest Louisville](/images/blog/bockfest-goat-racing-louisville.jpg)

The main attraction is the goat racing. Twelve races throughout the day, with goats supplied by Sunny Acres Farm and the Jefferson County Farm Bureau running the show. The highlight is the baby goat race where Louisville TV anchors and meteorologists each pick a baby goat to champion, and the winner's charity gets a $500 donation. It's exactly as fun as it sounds.

![NuLu Bockfest goat race crowd](/images/blog/nulu-bockfest-goat-race-crowd.jpg)

## The Scene

Beyond the goat track, the rest of the festival is wall-to-wall good times. Local breweries pouring their exclusive bock beers, Bulleit Bourbon specialty cocktails, food trucks, and the Wurst Fest where local chefs go head to head for the title of Best of the Wurst. There's a petting zoo, a bubble truck, and a tipping tractor for the kids. The NuLu 502 fun run kicks things off in the morning - a 502-foot "micro marathon" down Clay Street.

## NuLu Is Worth the Trip

If you've never spent a day in NuLu, you're missing out. AFAR magazine named it one of the best food neighborhoods in the country, and once you walk through you'll understand why. The restaurants, the shops, the street art, the energy - it's the kind of neighborhood that makes Louisville feel like a much bigger city than it is. And when Bockfest takes over, it's on another level.
